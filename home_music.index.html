✨ How to use HOME MUSIC

1. Tap Add downloaded music and choose audio files from Downloads or your music folder.

2. Tap ▶ beside a song to play it. Search to find a track, or use Previous / Next.

3. Add your own shayari. Your saved songs and poems remain in this browser unless its site data is cleared.

Tip: this is a local personal music player, not a music downloader. Only add music files you have the right to use.
